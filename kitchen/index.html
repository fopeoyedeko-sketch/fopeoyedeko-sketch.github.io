      Your week

      Home workout, 30 minutes

      No gym needed. Do 3 rounds and rest 1 minute between rounds. Every 2 weeks, add 2 reps to each move.

      1. Squats to a chair, 12
      2. Press-ups, 8 (knees or against the wall is fine)
      3. Reverse lunges, 10 each leg
      4. Glute bridges, 15
      5. Rows with a full backpack, 12
      6. Plank, 20 seconds

      The 7 rules

      1. Batch-cook on Sunday. Lunch is always in the box.
      2. Half the plate is vegetables, and rice, pasta or swallow is one fist.
      3. Have protein at breakfast: eggs or yoghurt.
      4. No juice or fizzy drinks. Water or no-added-sugar squash.
      5. Keep a snack box in the car: fruit and a boiled egg.
      6. One treat a week from the shop (doughnut, brioche, biscuits), and one planned takeaway a month.
      7. Weigh in every Sunday morning and send it to Akọ̀wé.
